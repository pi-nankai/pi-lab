Faculty Members

Chongyi Li  李重仪

Professor  ·  Nankai University

Chongyi Li is a Professor with the School of Computer Science, Nankai University, China. Before joining Nankai, he was a Research Assistant Professor from 2021 to 2023 with the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He was a research fellow with the City University of Hong Kong (supervised by J. K. Lee Chair Prof. Sam Kwong) and Nanyang Technological University (supervised by President's Chair Prof. Chen Change Loy) from 2018 to 2021. He received his Ph.D. degree (supervised by Prof. Jichang Guo) from Tianjin University in 2018. He was also a joint training Ph.D. student (supervised by Prof. Fatih Porikli) at Australian National University, Australia.

Chunle Guo  郭春乐

Associate Professor  ·  Nankai University

Chun-Le Guo is an Associate Professor at the College of Computer Science, Nankai University, China. He received his PhD degree from Tianjin University in 2020. He conducted the Ph.D. research as a Visiting Student with the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Queen Mary University of London (QMUL), UK. Then He continued his research as a Research Associate with the Department of Computer Science, City University of Hong Kong (CityU of HK).

Jieyu Yuan  袁捷禹

Postdoctoral Researcher  ·  Nankai University

Jieyu Yuan works as a Post-Doctoral Researcher in Nankai University. His research interests include remote sensing image processing, underwater image enhancement, and object detection.

Ming-Ming Cheng  程明明

Professor  ·  Nankai University

Ming-Ming Cheng is a professor and doctoral supervisor at Nankai University, and the director of the Media Computing Lab. He received the IEEE TPAMI Distinguished Reviewer award, the IBM Global SUR award, etc. He is a senior member of the IEEE and on the editorial boards of the IEEE TPAMI and IEEE TIP.
